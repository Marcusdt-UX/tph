anke — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

anke is a private lending-circle and personal-finance app operated by Thomas Publishing House, LLC, a Michigan limited liability company ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. anke is currently in a closed friends-and-family pilot in Michigan.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name and email address, used to sign you in and identify you to other members of circles you join.
  • Lending activity — circles you create or join, contributions, loans, votes, and repayments you record in the app.
  • Wallet data — your blockchain wallet address and USDC balance on the Base network. Your wallet is created on your own device through our wallet provider. Its key stays on your device; anke never holds it and cannot move your money.
  • Group fund activity — deposits, loans, votes and repayments in a group's fund happen on the public Base network. They are recorded there under wallet addresses, not names, and anyone can see them.
  • Linked bank data (optional) — if you connect a bank through our data provider (SimpleFIN), we receive read-only account balances and transaction history to power budgeting and cash-flow features. We never receive your bank login credentials.
  • Financial details you enter — budgets, goals, debts, and loans you add manually.
  • Push notification token — so we can alert you about votes, payments, and money arriving. You can disable notifications in your device settings.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal or financial data to anyone.
  • We do not show third-party advertising.
  • We do not collect or store bank passwords, card numbers, or wallet private keys. Buying USDC and cashing out to your bank are handled entirely by our payment partner, which also checks your identity; we don't receive your ID documents. Bank connections are handled by SimpleFIN.

How your data is used

Solely to provide the app: run your circles and lending book, keep your balance and transactions accurate, sync linked bank data you asked us to connect, send you relevant notifications, and improve reliability and security. The optional in-app assistant ("Ask anke") sends a summary of your own financial figures to an AI model provider to answer your questions; it is used only to generate your answer.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers that make the app work: Supabase (secure database and backend hosting), our wallet provider (your wallet), our payment partner (adding money and cashing out), SimpleFIN (bank data you connect), Expo (push notifications), and our AI model provider (only when you use Ask anke). Each processes data only to deliver its part of the service. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by emailing us at the address below; we will delete it except where we must retain records to comply with law. On-chain transactions recorded on the public Base network cannot be deleted.

Security

Data is protected with encryption in transit and row-level access controls, so you can only see your own and your circle members' relevant data. Your money moves only when you approve it on your own device. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children

anke is not directed to anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be noted by updating the date above.
